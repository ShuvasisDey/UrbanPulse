# 🏙️ UrbanPulse: Smart City Management System

![UrbanPulse Banner](https://img.shields.io/badge/Status-Active-brightgreen) ![PHP](https://img.shields.io/badge/PHP-8.x-777BB4?logo=php) ![MySQL](https://img.shields.io/badge/MySQL-Database-4479A1?logo=mysql) ![UI](https://img.shields.io/badge/UI-Glassmorphism-0ea5e9)

**UrbanPulse** is a comprehensive, centralized Smart City Management System built with PHP and MySQL. It integrates essential city services—such as healthcare, traffic control, energy monitoring, and citizen grievances—into a single, unified, beautifully designed dashboard.

## ✨ Key Features & Modules

The system is powered by a multi-role authentication architecture and is divided into several specialized portals:

### 👤 Citizen Portal
* **Unified Dashboard:** Citizens can track their city interactions from one place.
* **Smart Healthcare:** Book hospital appointments and view medical status.
* **Energy & Billing:** Monitor monthly energy usage and track utility bills.
* **Traffic Integration:** View registered vehicles and track outstanding traffic violation fines.
* **City Feedback:** Raise complaints (e.g., broken streetlights, potholes) and provide direct feedback to city administration.

### 👑 Central Manager (Admin)
* Global oversight of all city modules.
* Approve new Citizen registration requests.
* Send city-wide notifications and announcements.
* Generate high-level analytical reports.

### 🏥 Hospital Manager & Doctors
* **Hospital Manager:** Manage patient admissions and medical staff across city hospitals.
* **Doctor Portal:** View upcoming appointments, manage patient treatment statuses, and track salary ledgers.

### 🚦 Traffic & Energy Managers
* **Traffic Control:** Register city vehicles, log traffic violations, and issue fines.
* **Energy Grid:** Manage residential/commercial energy accounts, log monthly unit consumption, and generate digital utility bills.
